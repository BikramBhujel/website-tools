Wi-Fi AP Planner

Estimate access-point count from both RF coverage and client capacity, then generate a preliminary ceiling-placement grid.

LOCAL CALCULATION

Site inputs

Use indoor dimensions and design targets. Results are preliminary and must be validated with a predictive or on-site RF survey.

What this planner does not model

Wall materials, furniture, neighboring WLANs, channel reuse, transmit power, antenna patterns, client radio differences, roaming, DFS events and real spectrum utilization are not simulated.

Planning result

The recommendation is the higher of the coverage requirement and capacity requirement.

Preliminary placement grid

Use this only as a starting pattern. Move APs after reviewing walls, rooms, obstructions, cabling and RF survey results.

Design guidance

CoverageUse a target edge RSSI appropriate to the application. Tighter RSSI targets generally require more APs.
CapacityClient count alone is not airtime demand. High-throughput or voice-heavy environments may need a lower client-per-AP design limit.
PlacementAvoid blindly centering APs in corridors. Place radios where users are, while accounting for wall attenuation and cell overlap.
ValidationFinalize with a predictive design and post-install validation survey using the actual AP model, channel plan and transmit power.
